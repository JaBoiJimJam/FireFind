FireFind

FireFind is an open-source firewall risk identification tool developed by Triskele Labs. It analyzes firewall configurations from multiple vendors to identify high-risk rules, security vulnerabilities, and compliance issues. Using advanced pattern recognition, FireFind instantly detects exposed admin ports, overly permissive rules, and potential attack vectors across your entire firewall infrastructure.
